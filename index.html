Frequently asked questions

Useful answers before we start.

What type of organizations do you work with?

We support NGOs, trusts, charities, service businesses and growing organizations that need practical digital solutions.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. We can review the current design, mobile experience, content hierarchy, performance and enquiry journey, then recommend targeted improvements.

Can you integrate a payment gateway?

Yes. Integration can be planned for approved providers such as Cashfree or Razorpay after the organization completes the provider's merchant onboarding and compliance requirements.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Maintenance, content updates, issue resolution and planned enhancements can be included based on the agreed support scope.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on the number of pages or workflows, integrations, content readiness, complexity and ongoing support. Share your requirements for a scoped estimate.

How long does a project take?

A focused landing page can be faster than a full website, payment journey or custom application. A realistic schedule is confirmed after discovery and scope agreement.
