Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Information we receive

When a visitor contacts IBI SOLUTION, the visitor may provide a name, email address, phone number and project information. The current website forms prepare an email in the visitor's email application; the website does not itself store form submissions in a database.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, understand project requirements, prepare proposals and provide requested services. Information should not be sold or used for unrelated marketing without permission.

Third-party services

The website may link to WhatsApp, email applications, GitHub Pages and approved payment or analytics services. Those services apply their own privacy terms. Payment details should be collected only by the selected payment provider, not through a normal contact form.

Retention and security

Enquiry correspondence should be retained only as long as necessary for business, contractual or legal needs. Reasonable access controls should be used for business email and associated systems.

Your choices

A person may ask IBI SOLUTION to correct or delete enquiry information, subject to applicable legal and contractual requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact contact@ibisolution.in.

Important: Replace the temporary email address and review this policy with a qualified legal professional before commercial launch, especially if analytics, advertising cookies, newsletters, payment processing or customer accounts are added.
